Weekly Waste Segregation Inspection
HSE Section – Waste Management & Environmental Compliance








1. Waste Bins & Labelling

Correct number of waste bins available
Recycling bins available where required
Bins clearly labelled by waste type
Bin labels clean and readable

2. Segregation Compliance

General waste bins free from recyclables
Recycling bins contain correct materials only
Food waste disposed correctly (if applicable)
No hazardous items in normal bins

3. Storage, Cleanliness & Odour

Bins clean and free from contamination
Waste stored in bags/liners
Bins not overflowing; lids close properly
Area around bins clean with no spills
No strong or unpleasant odours

4. Collection & Transport

Collection schedule respected
Proper tools used to transport waste
Internal routes clean and unobstructed
External waste area tidy and organised

5. Safety & Environmental Practices

Housekeeping staff use PPE when handling waste
No evidence of pests near waste areas
No leaking bags or liquid waste
Fire exits and emergency routes not blocked by waste

6. Awareness & Behaviour

Students and staff use correct bins
Awareness posters/signage visible
Housekeeping team briefed this week
